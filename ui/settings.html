Type instead of paste
Saywell pastes text through the clipboard (and then puts your clipboard back). For apps where pasting doesn't work, like some terminals or remote desktops, it can type the text instead. Use the program's name; the History window shows it for each dictation.
    Clean up text with local AI
    Removes "um", repeats and false starts, and fixes punctuation. Runs on your graphics card; turning it off frees about 3.5 GB of video memory.
    Show words while speaking
    Your latest words above the floating bar, so you can see how far you got. The final text is worked out again when you finish.
